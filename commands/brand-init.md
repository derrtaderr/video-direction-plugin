# /brand-init

`/brand-init` will interview you in mostly A/B picks — world (light/dark), type
register, accent color and its scarcity rule, mascot policy or none, audio defaults —
and write a complete `motion-brand.md` into your project, then render a single proof
styleframe in your new brand so you see "that's MY brand" before you make a real video.
Taste by comparison, never "describe your brand."

**Not yet implemented.** For now, write `motion-brand.md` by hand from the template at
`templates/motion-brand-template.md`. The sections it needs:

- **Constants** — world/background, type, texture, logo, register (kept on every video)
- **Styles / roster** — your named looks, each tied to a message type
- **Selection rules** — how to pick a style when the message is ambiguous
- **Audio** — bed/VO defaults and whether silent is permitted
- **Delivery** — aspect, size budget, sound-off legibility

Once `motion-brand.md` exists in your project, ask for a video in plain language and the
`video-direction` skill takes it from there.
