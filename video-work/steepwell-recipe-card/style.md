# Style direction — steepwell-recipe-card

**Style from the roster:** Announcement Card (Steepwell roster #4).
**Kind: static** → hierarchy check.

**Returning-brand ledger read (the compounding story).** `brand/style-ledger.md` has one
row — winter-oolong-launch, 2026-07-28: Launch Film, silent, push-in camera, amber
turn-word ("slower"), amber underline on the "three minutes" proof, staggered type
reveal. This piece rotates every axis against that row, cited per the skill's stage-2
rule: last piece was a narrative Launch Film with a push-in — going static Announcement
Card with a held camera (none); last accent move was a turn-word plus underline — going
accent-on-the-punchline (the steep time), no underline; last look was type on the bare
paper world — going a physical recipe-card prop ON the world; and the card's charm slot
(untried for this brand) gets its first use.

**Why this embodies the ask on-brand.** The brand file's constants already contain the
recipe book: warm paper world `#F5F3EE`, subtle paper grain, warm editorial serif, calm
unhurried register. The card leans into them — an aged-cream clipped card (a PROP on the
world, so the world constant holds), a dashed ink cut-out border, ruled lines, an ink
botanical sprig, a hand-drawn steam curl. Nothing off-file was needed; "recipe book" is a
texture-and-layout treatment of the existing constants, not a new world.

**Signature moves (3 chosen, per the archetype):**
1. **The card is the frame.** One held aged-paper recipe card, slightly askew like a
   clipping pasted into a book; dashed ink border, ruled lines, a "No. 12" corner mark.
2. **One quiet charm.** The ink teacup's steam curl draws itself once (stroke draw), the
   card's only ornament in motion. Everything else is still.
3. **Amber reserved for the punchline.** The steep time ("3 minutes, unhurried") lands
   LAST in the reading order as the frame's single amber element.

**Variant axes (Announcement Card):** layout → left-aligned recipe layout (not centered);
charm element → the steam curl (first use); accent target → the steep time (punchline);
aspect → square.

**Logo-exemption resolution (stated per the brand file).** The wordmark's amber "well"
would sit beside the amber steep time on the card frame — so "Steepwell" holds
near-black/neutral for the whole card, and the amber "well" appears only on the final
dark resolve frame, where it is the single accent. Exactly the brand file's rule.

**One ease system.** power2.out settles for every entrance (card drop, line staggers);
the steam draw is a single stroke-dashoffset tween on the same ease.
