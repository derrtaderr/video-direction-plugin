# Steepwell — Motion Brand System

> A seasonal loose-leaf tea subscription for people who left tea bags behind and want the ritual without the guesswork.

## Constants (every video keeps these)

- **World.** Background `#F5F3EE` (warm paper, calm and editorial — reads like a well-set page). Dark closing frame in `#20242B` allowed for one emphasis moment (the wordmark resolve), off by default per beat.
- **Accent.** `#C6862E` (honey-amber, the color of oolong in the cup) is THE accent — it carries meaning (the key word, the steep time, the CTA), never decoration. Scarcity rule: **Strict — exactly one accent element per frame** `(defaulted)`.
- **Type.** Headings in a warm editorial serif, body in a clean sans. Headlines short, no orphan words.
- **Texture.** Subtle paper grain matched to the warm world; never busy.
- **Logo / wordmark.** "Steepwell" set in the heading serif; the "**well**" carries the honey-amber accent, the rest near-black. Every video ends on it with `steepwell.co`. _Logo-exemption:_ the amber "well" is identity, exempt from the one-accent-per-frame count, EXCEPT on any frame where it would sit beside a second accent use (then hold "well" neutral/near-black until the final resolve frame, where it becomes the single accent).
- **Register.** Calm, precise, unhurried — like a trusted tea buyer, not a tech pitch. Banned words: no "revolutionary", no "disrupt", no "game-changing".

## Styles / roster (pick by message type)

1. **Launch Film** — new box / seasonal launch / milestone. 20–40s on the warm paper field, serif headline, honey-amber on the one turn word. Cold-open hook → the tea-bag rut it answers → the box revealed → one proof (origin, harvest) → wordmark resolve. Signature move: eased hero push-in on the box reveal. Ends on the Steepwell wordmark (amber "well") + `steepwell.co`; dark closing frame optional for the resolve. [narrative]
2. **Mechanism Explainer** — how the box works or how a tea steeps, step by step. 20–45s, one panel per step on paper, near-black cards, honey-amber tracing the active step (the steep timer, the water line). Held camera on composed panels; motion is content-level. [narrative]
3. **Kinetic Essay** — a point of view on tea and ritual. 15–30s, serif kinetic type on the paper field, one honey-amber word per beat carrying the turn, cuts on the bed's rhythm. [narrative]
4. **Announcement Card** — a ship date / price / one CTA, one held frame. ≤12s, square, static card (Steepwell wordmark top → the one fact → supporting details → CTA landing last), honey-amber reserved for the punchline, one quiet charm animation. Ends on the wordmark. [static]
5. **Content Hero** — a quote, stat, or note repurposed for the feed. 8–20s, hero-scale serif line on paper, honey-amber on the load-bearing word, subtle brand frame. Reads with sound off. [static]

_Each entry is tagged narrative or static:_ narrative styles get the story-spine check; static/stacked-info styles get the lighter hierarchy check.
