# Look critic — launch-film hero styleframes (STAGE 4 STOP)

The hero moment is the three-exhibits beat (boards thesis). Two fully-designed directions
of the SAME moment, rendered as stills, judged against the Launch Film canon
(`references/canon-deck.md` §1, the Claude Code in-app browser launch: warm textured field,
floating rounded windows never fullscreen, dark/light doing the labeling, camera as
narration, wordmark resolve) and the brand constants in `motion-brand.md`. No local canon
frame ships in the repo (the teardown links a public video), so the comparison is against
the teardown verbatim.

**A/B rule:** stills only, no two videos rendered. This is a flagship piece, so the pick is
Jason's — the critic reports, it does not decide.

---

## Frame A — gallery-dark triptych · `styleframe-A-gallery-dark.png`

Warm charcoal wall, faint grain. Three framed exhibit clips hung in a row, each carrying
its own color (Ledgerline green "Tax-ready.", Steepwell serif "Winter Oolong", Fern green
"$24/mo"). Bone serif thesis line above with one bone accent ("each in its own look.").
Mono placards under each frame; a muted mono sub-line below.

- **Anti-slop:** no static centered single window — three framed works, and the film punches
  a camera into each on its beat. Pass.
- **Accent discipline:** exactly one house accent per frame (the bone phrase "each in its
  own look."), carrying the thesis turn — meaning, not decoration. The exhibits' green/amber
  are the SUBJECTS on the wall (paintings), not the gallery's accent, which is the whole
  point of the hueless house. Pass.
- **Canon fidelity:** warm textured field ✓, floating rounded windows never fullscreen ✓,
  dark wall / light exhibits = the canon's dark/light value labeling ✓ (rule 8). This is the
  Launch Film register almost line for line. Strong pass.
- **Brand constants:** holds the neutral-dark World, the value-accent, serif-headline /
  mono-label discipline, one-accent scarcity. On-brand.
- **Notes for build (not blockers):** Georgia is a system-default serif — map to a more
  distinctive editorial serif at build via HyperFrames font mapping. The thesis line could
  drop ~4px so the triptych owns more of the frame. Ledgerline (pure white) and Steepwell /
  Fern (cream) read close in value against the wall — the distinctness lands through type
  and accent, which is enough, but a hair more warmth on the wall would separate the whites.

**Verdict: PASS. On-canon for Launch Film, on-brand, and it does the film's job — three
brands hung like works, the house receding to frame them. Recommended pick.**

## Frame B — light editorial stack · `styleframe-B-light-editorial.png`

Bright parchment field. Left: a large serif headline ("Three brands. Not one designer."),
a mono kicker, a serif lede. Right: the three exhibits as a numbered editorial contents
list (01/02/03), each a small thumbnail with a serif name, a mono descriptor, and the
persona's plain-language line in italic. A hairline rule splits the columns.

- **Anti-slop:** clean hierarchy, clear reading order (kicker → headline → lede → numbered
  list), no default-centered dead frame. Pass.
- **Accent discipline:** no house accent hue (light field); meaning is carried by scale and
  the italic turn-word "designer." The exhibits keep their own color as thumbnails. No
  competing accents. Pass on discipline.
- **Canon fidelity:** here it departs. The canon Launch Film is a warm DARK textured field
  with floating windows; B inverts to a light editorial page and shrinks the exhibits to
  contents-list thumbnails. It reads closer to an editorial poster / Mechanism-Explainer
  register than the launch-film canon, and the exhibits lose the "works on a wall" drama —
  they become citations, not the subjects. Off-canon for this archetype.
- **Brand constants:** it BREAKS the committed neutral-dark World constant (light field) and
  softens the gallery framing. Choosing B is a decision to amend `motion-brand.md`'s World
  to a light editorial house, not just a frame pick.

**Verdict: PASS as design, but off-canon for Launch Film and off the brand's neutral-dark
World. It is the genuinely strong alternative — a lighter, print-editorial house — not a
strawman. Pick it only if you want to re-point the house toward light editorial (amend the
World constant), accepting that the exhibits read as citations rather than framed works.**

## The choice for Jason

- **A** keeps the house a dark gallery that frames the work (canon-true, exhibits dominant).
- **B** re-points the house to a light editorial page (exhibits as a contents list).

Both are honest, strong directions. The critic's recommendation is **A** for canon fidelity
and because the film's stated job is exhibiting three brands with the house receding — A does
that, B competes less but reframes them as references. This is the stage-4 A/B gate; the
build waits on the pick. Re-roll bound: 0 used.
