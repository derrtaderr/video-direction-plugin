# Treatment — launch-film

**The ONE idea.** Every AI-made video looks the same because nothing in the tools forces a
look decision. You type make me a launch video, and you get the statistical average of
everything the model has seen. This film answers that with evidence you can see in one
frame. Three real brands walk through, and not one of them has a designer. A bookkeeping
app that wanted to feel clean and not techy. A tea company that asked to feel like a
well-worn recipe book. A garden-supply shop that sounds like a good garden-center
employee. Each described its brand in plain words with zero design vocabulary, and one free
plugin turned those words into a look, then directed a launch video in it. Same system,
three brands that could not be mistaken for each other. The film hangs them like a gallery,
a quiet dark room with the three works framed on the wall, each getting its own moment,
then the room resolves to one line telling you the system is a plugin you install and run
on your own machine.

**Arc.** Open on the problem stated flat on a dark wall (the slop, in under 2 seconds).
Turn on the reason nobody names (nothing forced a look decision). Reveal three framed
exhibits, each brand getting a distinct punch-in moment with its plain-language fact. Land
on one system, then resolve to the wordmark and the install line.

**Runtime budget.** ~42s master (≤45s), 16:9 1920×1080.

**Audio mode.** Silent, type-led (permitted by `motion-brand.md`; the framed exhibits and
the on-screen type carry the piece; the hook lands inside 2s either way). One-line note on
the render: silent per brand; add a low gallery-room bed at `composition/audio/` to score
it.

**Destinations.** README / lab hero (16:9 master) + LinkedIn native + X native (1:1 cut
from the master, per `delivery-matrix.md`). The 1:1 cut is produced later, after the A/B
pick and build.

**Style pick.** Launch Film — kind: narrative → story-spine check runs at boards.
