# Style direction — Seedling Subscription Launch Card

**Style from the roster:** Announcement Card (Fern & Field's "Launch Card"). **Kind: static**
→ hierarchy check.

**HyperFrames route:** `/motion-graphics` — a short, unnarrated, motion-first card. The
lighter route; build directly against the composition contract, no narrative ceremony.

**Signature moves (2–3 chosen):**
1. A single **charm animation** — a seedling sprouting (two leaves unfurling from a stem).
   Nothing else on the card moves.
2. The **fern-green accent reserved for the punchline** — the price `$24/mo`. It is the only
   green meaning-bearing element on its frame.
3. **Generous air** and a top-down reading order so the eye lands on the wordmark first and
   the price last.

**Variant axes chosen (so repeat cards vary deliberately):**
- Layout: **centered**.
- Charm element: **sprouting seedling** (vs a leaf unfurl or price settle).
- Accent target: **price** (vs headline or CTA).
- Aspect: **square** 1080×1080.

**Accent / logo-exemption resolution.** The wordmark's green "&" and the green price would
share the card, which the brand's logo-exemption forbids (two greens competing). Resolution
per the constant: the wordmark "&" is held **neutral (deep moss #22291F)** on every frame
where the green price is present, and turns **green only on the final resolve frame**, where
the price has receded to neutral and the "&" is the single accent. Stated, not left to chance.

**Ease system:** one gentle ease reused for every entrance — `power2.out`, ~0.7s, staggered.
