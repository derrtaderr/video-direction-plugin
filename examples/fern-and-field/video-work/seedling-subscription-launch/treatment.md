# Treatment — Seedling Subscription Launch Card

**The one idea.** Fern & Field is launching the Seedling Subscription — native plant plugs
delivered monthly — for $24/mo, starting March 1. This is one fact to land on one held
frame: the offer, its price, its start date, a way to act.

**Message type → style.** A date + price + single CTA. Per the brand's selection rules this
maps to the **Announcement Card** (Fern & Field's "Launch Card"). Kind: **static** — so the
**hierarchy check** runs at boards, not the story-spine check. (Confirmed against the skill's
Structure-check section: static / stacked-info styles get the lighter hierarchy check; a card
is not making a but/therefore argument.)

**Arc.** Not a narrative arc — a reading order on a held card. Wordmark settles top → serif
headline names the thing → a tidy detail list → the price lands LAST in fern green → close on
the wordmark + fernandfield.com. One charm animation (a seedling sprouting) supplies the
motion; everything else is still.

**Runtime budget.** ~11s (Announcement Card is ≤12s).

**Audio mode.** Silent. The brand's Audio line permits silent explicitly ("Silent permitted?
Yes"), and no bed is on hand, so per the skill's **Audio precedence** rule the brand file
wins over the general audio floor: render silent, note it. Card is fully legible with sound
off.

**Destination.** Fern & Field social feed (square, muted autoplay).
