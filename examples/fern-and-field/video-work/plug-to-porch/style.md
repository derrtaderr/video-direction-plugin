# Style direction — plug-to-porch

**Style from the roster:** Mechanism Explainer. Kind: **narrative** → story-spine check.

**HyperFrames route:** built directly against the composition contract (one `index.html`,
one clip per beat, a persistent route map over the clips).

**Ledger read.** The last two rows are the Announcement Card (silent, one held frame) and
the Seedling Subscription launch film (VO-led, a camera that travels, a dark closing
frame). This piece goes the other way from the film on every axis that both share: captions
instead of VO, panels that hold their framing while the content moves, the accent tracing a
path instead of marking a word, and a close on the paper world.

**Signature moves (2–3 chosen):**
1. `control-target-sync` — a sun crosses the sky and the plugs answer by growing; the
   control drives the target, which is the mechanism in one picture.
2. `card-morph-anchor` — the plug is wrapped: the paper sleeve rises around the root ball
   from an anchor at its base, and the box closes over it.
3. `typewriter-reveal` — each step's label types into the route map as the plant arrives
   there, so the map writes itself as the story goes.

**Variant axes chosen:**
- Panel layout: **traveling** (a route map stays put while the station panel below changes).
- VO vs captions: **captions**, one sentence per beat.
- Step count: **3** (seed, grow, ship), plus setup and payoff.
- Accent role: **path-tracer** (the route line traces in fern green from stop to stop).

**Accent plan (one green per frame).** The only green is the traced segment of the route
line. On the resolve, the route line fades to neutral and the wordmark "&" takes the
green. Plants are sage `#8FA97E`, never the accent.

**Ease system:** `power3.out` at 0.6s for every entrance, staggered; camera on `sine.inOut`.
