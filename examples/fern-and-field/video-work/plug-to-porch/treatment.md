# Treatment — plug-to-porch

**The ONE idea.** A Fern & Field plant is grown from your region's seed, outdoors, and
shipped in your planting window, so it goes in the ground the week it should.

**Arc.** Setup: the box on the porch, and the question of how the plant inside got there.
Step 1: it starts as seed gathered in your region. Therefore step 2: it grows up outdoors,
in your region's weather. Therefore step 3: it ships in a paper sleeve, timed to your
planting window. Payoff: it goes in the ground the week it should. A route map across the
top tracks the plant from stop to stop.

**Runtime budget.** ~28.5s (Mechanism Explainer is 20–45s).

**Audio mode.** Bed with captions. The archetype allows "a bed with captions where the
words carry the mechanism", and the brand's default is type-led with no VO. One caption
sentence per beat is the whole script (caption-as-narrator, from the Vaults teardown).

**Destinations.** Plugin sample (README, square social).

**Style pick.** Mechanism Explainer — how a product works, step by step. Kind:
**narrative**, so the story-spine check runs at boards. Anchor: the Vaults feature clip in
`references/canon-deck.md`.

**Facts on screen.** The four process steps are this fictional example brand's own
materials, written here for the example: seed gathered in your region, grown outdoors, a
paper sleeve, shipped in your planting window. No numbers appear.
