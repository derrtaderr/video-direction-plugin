sentence per beat (caption-as-narrator). A route map runs across the top for the whole
piece: Seed · Grow · Ship · Plant, the line between stops tracing in fern green as the
plant travels.

**Motion signature.** The map stays still and the plant moves through it: every beat ends
with the green line one stop further along, and the camera only leans in on the step being
explained.

**Story spine.** The box is at your door (setup), **but** where did the plant come from? It
started as your region's seed (step 1), **therefore** it grows up outdoors in your weather
(step 2), **therefore** it can ship in a paper sleeve the moment your planting window opens
(step 3), **therefore** it goes in the ground the week it should (payoff). Each step is the
reason the next one works.

**Timing critic.**
- Hook inside 2s: the box and the first route stop are on screen by 0.6s, the caption by 0.8s. ✓
- Each caption (8–11 words) stays settled 3.0s or more, past the 0.3s per word floor. ✓
- Total 28.5s, inside the 20–45s budget. ✓
- Stranger test: one viewing tells a stranger what it is (native plants grown and shipped
  for you), who it is for (home gardeners) and how to get it (fernandfield.com). ✓

**Accent count.** The traced route segment is the only green until the resolve, where the
route fades to neutral before the "&" goes green. Plants are sage.

**Swaps.** None; every visual is drawn and the brand is fictional.
