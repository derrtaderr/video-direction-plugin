# Styleframe check — routine piece

A routine piece gets one styleframe pass, self-checked against the look rubric and the
Mechanism Explainer's anchor (the Vaults feature clip in `references/canon-deck.md`), not an
A/B pair.

`build-pass/contact-sheet-1.jpg` and `-2.jpg` are the first full pass. Two fixes came out of
reading them, shown in `contact-sheet-fix.jpg`:

- Beat 2 was a bare tray, so nothing said where the seed came from. A "Local seed" stake
  now springs up beside it.
- Beat 4's standing flaps were the same kraft as the box front, so the open box read as a
  tall closed one. The flaps are lighter and splayed open, then close flat.

Anchor check: like the Vaults clip, the frame holds while the content moves (seeds fall,
plugs grow, a sleeve rises), and a single accent marks the semantic moment, here the route
line tracing to each stop.
