# Styleframe pick — the hero frame (beat 3, after the reveal locks)

Flagship piece, so two directions of the same moment, judged against the Launch Film's
restrained anchor (the Claude Code in-app browser launch in `references/canon-deck.md`).

- **A (`hero-A.png`)** — centered stack: title over the open box, the bloom on the frame's
  center line, the month card tucked at the front left.
- **B (`hero-B.png`)** — editorial split: the title set left in three lines, the box carried
  right.

**Picked A.** In B the third title line runs into the box's left flap and the box crowds the
right edge, two fixes before it is even a fair comparison. A also lands where the reveal
starts: the pull-back begins on the bloom at frame center, so in A the bloom never leaves the
center line and the move reads as one gesture; in B it would drift right on the way out.

Honest note on order: the frame was built first and B was rendered from it, so stages 4 and
5 overlapped here. `build-pass/` holds the stills the build was directed from.
