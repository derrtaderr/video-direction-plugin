# Style direction — seedling-subscription-film

**Style from the roster:** Launch Film. Kind: **narrative** → story-spine check.

**HyperFrames route:** product-launch weight, built directly against the composition
contract (one `index.html`, one clip per beat). No site capture; every visual is drawn.

**Ledger read.** The only shipped row is the Announcement Card: centered, silent, one
seedling charm, camera a single push, accent on the price, paper world throughout. This
piece moves every one of those: a five-scene narrative instead of one held frame, VO-led
instead of silent, the camera travelling scene to scene, the accent doing the turn word
instead of the price, and the close on the dark moss world instead of paper.

**Signature moves (2–3 chosen):**
1. `zoom-out-workspace-reveal` — the hero reveal. Tight on the native plug that replaced a
   shelf pot, one decelerating pull-back reveals it seated in the Seedling Subscription box
   under the title.
2. `grid-card-assemble` — the visual rhyme. The shelf of identical pots and the calendar of
   twelve different species are the same grid; the first one is sameness, the second is
   the answer to it.
3. `blur-crossfade` — the one soft transition, into the dark resolve frame, where the world
   changes from paper to moss.

**Variant axes chosen:**
- Pace: **slow-burn** (holds for the VO, cuts on line boundaries).
- Voice: **VO-led** (Roger, ElevenLabs), with captions.
- Reveal style: **build-on** (the native plug grows out of the shelf pot, never cut-to).
- Closing-frame world: **dark emphasis frame** (`#22291F`), its first use in this brand.

**Accent plan (one green per frame).** Fern green `#2F6B4F` lands on:
beat 3 the caption word "natives" (the turn); beat 4 the March tile, the first month;
beat 5 the wordmark "&" only, with the price set in paper white. The meadow and the plugs
use sage naturals (`#8FA97E`), never the accent. The wordmark "&" stays neutral wherever
another green is on screen and goes green only on the resolve frame.

**Ease system:** one entrance ease reused everywhere, `power3.out` at 0.7s, staggered 0.08s;
camera moves on `sine.inOut`.

**Type.** Serif headings (Georgia, mapped by HyperFrames), system sans for captions and
labels, per the brand constants.
