# Treatment — seedling-subscription-film

**The ONE idea.** Native plants are hard to find, so Fern & Field grows them and sends you a
new one every month: the Seedling Subscription, $24/mo, starting March 1.

**Arc.** Cold-open tight on one native bloom drawing itself, then pulled back to a meadow of
plants that belong to one region (setup). But the garden center shelf is the same few pots,
coast to coast (turn). Therefore Fern & Field grows the natives itself: one pot on that shelf
becomes a native plug, and the camera pulls back to reveal it in the Seedling Subscription
box (reveal). The proof is the calendar: a new region-native species lands on every month
(evidence). Resolve on the dark moss frame with the wordmark, the price and the start date.

**Runtime budget.** ~30s (Launch Film is 20–40s).

**Audio mode.** VO over a bed. The brand allows a voice on a Launch Film "when narration
genuinely helps"; here it carries the but/therefore argument that type alone would have to
spell out at length. Bed: quiet acoustic folk per the brand's Audio default. Captions on
every VO line, because the brand's Delivery requires reading with sound off.

**Destinations.** Plugin sample (README hero, square social).

**Style pick.** Launch Film — a new product announced as the flagship piece. Kind:
**narrative**, so the story-spine check runs at boards. Anchor: the restrained Claude Code
in-app browser launch in `references/canon-deck.md` (the brand's register is warm and
unhurried, not energetic).

**Facts on screen.** Every product fact comes from the brand's own materials in this
example: native plant plugs delivered to your door, a new region-native species every
month, $24/mo, starts March 1. "Twelve species a year" is derived from one per month. Fern
& Field is fictional.
