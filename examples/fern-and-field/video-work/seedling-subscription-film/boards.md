**Motion signature.** The camera breathes in on each scene's point and out to its context
(glide out to the meadow, push in on one pot, pull back from the bloom to the box, lean in on
March), and the two grids rhyme: twelve of the same, then twelve different.

**Story spine.** Every region has its own plants (setup) **but** the garden center sells the
same few everywhere (turn), **therefore** we grow the natives and send them (thesis), and
the proof is a new species **every** month (evidence), **therefore** here is how to get it
(resolve). No "and then" joins: each beat answers the one before it.

**Timing critic.**
- Hook inside 2s: the coneflower is drawing at 0.0s and the first caption is up by 0.7s. ✓
- Every beat earns its seconds: each holds one VO line plus at least 2s of picture. ✓
- Cuts on VO boundaries: every line finishes at least 0.6s before its scene ends. ✓
- Total 31.5s, inside the 20–40s budget. ✓
- Stranger test: after one viewing a stranger knows what it is (a monthly native-plant
  subscription), who it is for (anyone planting a yard who can't find natives) and how to
  get it ($24/mo from March 1 at fernandfield.com). ✓

**Accent count.** One green per frame: beat 3's "natives", beat 4's March tile, beat 5's
"&". Beats 1 and 2 carry none, on purpose: the sameness has no accent to offer.

**Swaps.** None; every visual is drawn and the brand is fictional.
