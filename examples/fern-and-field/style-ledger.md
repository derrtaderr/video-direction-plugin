## Observations

- Three styles proven now: Announcement Card, Launch Film, Mechanism Explainer. Kinetic
  Essay and Content Hero are untried in this brand; the next piece should be one of them.
- The two narrative pieces both close on a pull-back from a coneflower. It worked twice; a
  third time is a habit. The next narrative piece should end on something else.
- The fern green "&" on the dark moss frame measures 2.38:1, under the 3:1 contrast floor.
  It reads, but quietly. If the dark resolve becomes common, the brand should decide whether
  the dark world carries a lighter accent tint, in `motion-brand.md`, not per piece.
